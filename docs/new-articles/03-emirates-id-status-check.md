## Article body

### [PARAGRAPH] Intro

Once your Emirates ID application is submitted, the waiting begins. Most applications are processed in 5–10 working days, but a small percentage get stuck — flagged for biometric verification, name mismatch with the passport, or a security review. The good news is you don't need to call anyone or visit an ICP centre to check the status. There are three reliable ways to track your Emirates ID application in 2026: the ICP website, the ICP UAE app, and SMS. This guide walks through each, explains every status code you might see, and tells you exactly what to do if your application is stuck.

### [PARAGRAPH] Quick takeaways bullet list

- The fastest way to check Emirates ID status is the **ICP UAE app** under "My Services" → "Status Check."
- You need your **application number** (also called PRAN or transaction number) — find it on the receipt from the typing centre or in the SMS you received after submission.
- Standard processing takes **5–10 working days**. New residents may need to attend biometrics first.
- If your status shows "Under process" for more than 10 working days, you have options to escalate.
- The physical Emirates ID card is delivered by Emirates Post to the address on the application — you can also collect it from the nearest post office if delivery fails.

## [H2] What information you need before checking

You need one of the following:

- **PRAN (Personal Reference Application Number)** — a 14-digit number on your typing centre receipt
- **Application number** — same as PRAN in most cases
- **Emirates ID number** — if you already have an EID and you're renewing
- **Passport number** — accepted as a fallback by some channels

You'll also need to know which **emirate** the application was submitted from. If you don't have any of these, check the SMS history on the phone number you provided — ICP usually sends a confirmation SMS with the application reference.

## [H2] Method 1: Check on the ICP website (works on any device)

This is the simplest method and works on phone or desktop.

1. Open [icp.gov.ae](https://icp.gov.ae/) in any browser.
2. Click **Services** → **Public Services** → **Check Your Status** (also reachable directly at `icp.gov.ae/en/services/check-your-status`).
3. Choose **Emirates ID** as the service type.
4. Enter your PRAN or application number.
5. Pass the captcha.
6. Tap **Search**.

The current status appears immediately. If it shows "Under process" for more than 10 working days, scroll down — there's often a sub-line explaining whether biometrics are pending, fees are unpaid, or a document is missing.

## [H2] Method 2: ICP UAE app (recommended for residents)

The **ICP UAE** app is the official Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security app. Available on iOS and Android.

1. Download **ICP UAE** from the App Store or Google Play. The publisher is "Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security."
2. Sign in with **UAE PASS** (recommended) or create an account with your passport details.
3. Tap **My Services** → **ID Card Status** or **Emirates ID Status**.
4. Enter your application number or use the auto-detect (if you signed in with UAE PASS, recent applications appear automatically).
5. Status appears with full history — when it moved from "Submitted" to "Under process" to "Approved" to "Card printed" to "Dispatched."

The app also sends push notifications when the status changes. If you do nothing else, install the app — you'll find out about issues faster than refreshing the website.

## [H2] Method 3: SMS check (no internet needed)

For Etisalat and Du customers:

- **Etisalat:** Send `EID` followed by a space and your application number to **2535**.
- **Du:** Send `EID` followed by a space and your application number to **2535**.

You'll receive a reply with the current status, usually within 30 seconds. This is useful if you're somewhere without reliable data — for example, attending a walk-in interview where you've been asked to provide your Emirates ID status on the spot.

## [H2] What each Emirates ID status means

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Application received, not yet picked up by ICP | Wait — usually moves within 24 hours |
| **Under process** | ICP is reviewing | Standard. Wait up to 10 working days. |
| **Biometrics required** | You need to attend an ICP centre | Book biometrics appointment via the app |
| **Documents required** | Something is missing or unclear | Log in and upload the requested document |
| **Approved** | Application approved, card being printed | Wait — card typically printed within 2–3 days |
| **Card printed** | Physical card is at Emirates Post | Wait for delivery (or arrange pickup) |
| **Dispatched** | Card sent to your address | Track on Emirates Post |
| **Delivered** | Card received | Done — pick it up |
| **Returned to sender** | Delivery failed, card back with Emirates Post | Pick up from the relevant post office within 30 days |
| **Rejected** | Application rejected | Reasons should be listed. Common: name mismatch with passport, expired passport, failed security check |

## [H2] What to do if your application is stuck

If "Under process" or "Biometrics required" hasn't moved in 10+ working days, here is the escalation order:

### [H3] Step 1: Re-check biometrics

The most common stuck-state is unscheduled biometrics. Even if you submitted online, ICP may flag the application for in-person biometric capture. Log in to the ICP app, check the status detail, and if biometrics are pending, book the appointment via the app at your nearest ICP centre. Bring your passport.

### [H3] Step 2: Call the ICP call centre

Dial **600 522222** (UAE toll number for ICP). The call centre is open Sunday to Thursday 7 AM to 9 PM and Saturday 8 AM to 5 PM. Give your application number — they can see exactly what is blocking the application and tell you the next step. English is supported.

### [H3] Step 3: Visit a Customer Happiness Centre

If the call centre can't unblock it, visit an ICP Customer Happiness Centre in your emirate. These are walk-in service centres (no appointment usually needed). Bring your passport, your application receipt, and a screenshot of the stuck status. A specialist can override most blocks in person.

### [H3] Step 4: Employer PRO escalation

If you're a new resident and your employer's PRO handled the original application, ask them to escalate. PROs have priority lines into ICP for employer-sponsored applications and can usually resolve issues in 1–2 working days.

## [H2] Common reasons applications get stuck

- **Name mismatch with passport.** The name on the EID application must match the passport exactly. Even a missing middle name or different transliteration of an Arabic name will flag the application.
- **Passport expiry under 6 months.** ICP rejects applications when the passport is close to expiring. Renew first.
- **Photo not meeting specs.** White background, neutral expression, both ears visible, no glasses (for some categories). Re-take and re-upload if requested.
- **Unpaid fees.** Some online applications pause if the fee transaction didn't clear. Re-attempt payment from the app.
- **Security check.** A small percentage of applications get a security flag — usually random sampling but sometimes related to background. These take longer and there is no way to expedite.

## [H2] How to find your Emirates ID card after it's printed

Once status shows "Card printed" or "Dispatched":

1. **Emirates Post delivery** is the default. Track via the [Emirates Post website](https://www.emiratespost.ae/) using the tracking number from the dispatch SMS.
2. **If delivery fails twice**, the card returns to the nearest post office and stays there for 30 days. After 30 days, ICP cancels the card and you must reapply (paying the fee again).
3. **Pickup from post office** — bring your passport. The post office staff will confirm your identity before handing the card over.

You can also choose **Emirates ID pickup at an ICP centre** during the application if you want to avoid postal delivery — useful if your residential address recently changed.

## [H2] Frequently Asked Questions

### [H3] How long does Emirates ID take to be issued?

Standard processing is 5–10 working days for a normal application. Renewals can be faster — sometimes 2–3 days. Stuck applications can take 3–4 weeks if biometrics or security review are involved.

### [H3] Can I work in the UAE before my Emirates ID is issued?

You can legally work as soon as your labour card is issued by MOHRE — Emirates ID is a separate document that follows. Most employers are fine with you starting before the physical EID card arrives, as long as the EID number has been issued (which happens immediately after biometrics).

### [H3] My Emirates ID expired and I haven't renewed. What happens?

Renew within 30 days of expiry to avoid the AED 20/day late fee (capped at AED 1,000). Beyond 90 days, your residence visa is at risk of cancellation. Use the ICP app to renew online — most renewals don't require a new biometric capture.

### [H3] Can I get a digital Emirates ID instead of the physical card?

The UAE PASS app shows your digital Emirates ID and is accepted at most government counters as proof of identity. Some private services (banks, telecom shops) still require the physical card.

### [H3] What if the EID number on the SMS doesn't match my Emirates ID?

This is rare but happens — usually a clerical issue. Visit an ICP Customer Happiness Centre with your passport and they'll regenerate the card with the correct number, free of charge.

### [H3] Can someone else collect my Emirates ID from the post office?

Yes, but they need a notarised power of attorney plus your original passport copy. Easier to redirect to a pickup point you can reach yourself.
