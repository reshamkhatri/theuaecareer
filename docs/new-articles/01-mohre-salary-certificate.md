### [PARAGRAPH] Intro

If you have ever applied for a UAE family visa, taken out a bank loan, opened a business account, or filed a visit visa for parents, you have probably been asked for a salary certificate from MOHRE. The catch is that there are actually two documents people call a "salary certificate" in the UAE — and one of them is increasingly being rejected by banks and embassies. This guide explains exactly what the MOHRE salary certificate is in 2026, how to download it from the MOHRE app or website, when you need the MOHRE version versus a bank-issued one, and what to do if the details on your certificate are wrong.

### [PARAGRAPH] Quick takeaways bullet list

- The **MOHRE salary certificate** is a digital document issued by the UAE Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation that confirms your registered employment, basic salary, and total package.
- It is **free** and can be downloaded in 60 seconds from the MOHRE app once your contract is active.
- A MOHRE salary certificate is accepted for **most government services** (Emirates ID, family visa, GDRFA matters) but some private banks now require an additional bank salary certificate from your salary-transfer bank.
- If your basic salary or allowance breakdown on the certificate is wrong, only your employer can fix it via a MOHRE contract amendment.
- The MOHRE certificate is in Arabic and English. There is no need to get a separate translation for UAE government use.

## [H2] What is a MOHRE salary certificate?

A MOHRE salary certificate is the official confirmation, issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, that you are a registered employee of a specific UAE establishment with a specific basic salary, housing allowance, transport allowance, and total monthly package. The certificate is generated automatically from the data your employer registered when they processed your work permit and MOHRE labour contract.

It contains:

- Your full name (as printed in your passport)
- Your nationality and passport number
- Your Emirates ID number
- Your employer name and MOHRE establishment number
- Your job title and contract type (fixed-term)
- Your contract start date
- The basic salary, housing allowance, transport allowance, and total monthly package
- The MOHRE QR verification code

The QR code matters. Any bank, embassy, or government counter can scan it to confirm the certificate is real and current. This is also why a screenshot or photo of someone else's certificate is useless for fraud — the QR code only verifies against MOHRE's live database.

## [H2] How to download your MOHRE salary certificate (2026 process)

You can get the certificate from three places: the MOHRE app, the MOHRE website, or a typing centre. The app is the fastest.

### [H3] Method 1: MOHRE app (fastest, free)

1. Download the **MOHRE - UAE** app on iOS or Android. The publisher is "Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation."
2. Sign in with **UAE PASS** or with your MOHRE username and password. If this is your first login, use the "Sign in with UAE PASS" option — almost everyone in the UAE already has UAE PASS linked to their Emirates ID.
3. From the home screen, scroll to **Services** and tap **Issue Salary Certificate** (sometimes labelled "Certificates" → "Salary Certificate").
4. Select the language: Arabic + English (default) is what most authorities want.
5. Confirm your details and tap **Generate**.
6. The certificate appears as a PDF. Save it to your phone or email it to yourself.

The whole process takes under a minute if your MOHRE record is up to date.

### [H3] Method 2: MOHRE website

1. Open [mohre.gov.ae](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/) in a browser.
2. Click **Services** → **Individuals** → **Salary Certificate**.
3. Sign in with UAE PASS.
4. Follow the same flow as the app.

### [H3] Method 3: Tas-heel typing centre

If you don't have a smartphone or your UAE PASS login is broken, any Tas-heel typing centre (there are hundreds across the UAE) can issue the certificate for you. Bring your Emirates ID. The fee at most centres is around AED 20–30. This route is rarely needed unless you have a technical problem.

## [H2] When you need a MOHRE salary certificate (and when you need a bank one instead)

This is where many residents get caught out. As of 2026, the rule of thumb is:

- **Use a MOHRE salary certificate for:** Emirates ID renewal, family visa applications via GDRFA/ICP, Schengen and US visa applications (most embassies), Tas-heel services, court cases involving labour disputes, and most rental contract submissions.
- **Use a bank-issued salary certificate for:** personal loan applications, credit card applications, car loan applications, mortgage applications, and any case where the lender wants confirmation of actual salary credits hitting your account.

The reason for the split: a MOHRE certificate confirms your contractual salary, but it does not confirm that the salary has actually been paid into your bank account. Lenders care about the latter because they want to see consistent salary credits before approving credit. Most UAE banks will issue a salary certificate against your salary-transfer account on request, usually free if you are a customer in good standing.

Some embassies (Schengen consulates in Dubai, for example) accept either, but they prefer the bank version if you are claiming a high salary. Always read the specific embassy checklist before deciding.

## [H2] What to do if your MOHRE salary certificate is wrong

If your basic salary, housing allowance, or job title on the certificate doesn't match your actual offer letter, the certificate itself cannot be edited — the underlying MOHRE contract record has to be amended first. Only your employer can do this.

The fix is:

1. Email your HR contact with the specific incorrect field and the correct value. Attach your offer letter as evidence.
2. HR submits a **Contract Amendment** request through the MOHRE Tas-heel system. This typically requires you to sign the amended contract in person or via UAE PASS.
3. Once MOHRE approves the amendment (usually 2–5 working days), regenerate your salary certificate. The new figure will appear.

If your employer refuses to correct a genuine error — especially if the certificate undervalues your basic salary, which then under-states your future gratuity — you can file a formal complaint with MOHRE via the [MOHRE call centre on 600 590 000](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/) or through the [Tawasul portal](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/). Read our guide on [how to file a MOHRE complaint in UAE](/blog/how-to-file-mohre-complaint-in-uae/) for the step-by-step.

## [H2] Common rejection reasons (and how to avoid them)

If a bank or embassy rejected your MOHRE salary certificate, the most common reasons are:

- **Certificate too old.** Most authorities want a certificate dated within the last 30 days. Regenerate it the day before submission.
- **Mismatch between certificate name and Emirates ID name.** This happens to people whose passport name was written differently when the contract was registered. The fix is a MOHRE amendment.
- **Embassy wants bank salary certificate.** Some Schengen consulates explicitly want bank-issued. Re-check the checklist.
- **QR code unverifiable.** This usually means the certificate is fake. A genuine MOHRE certificate always verifies — if yours doesn't, regenerate from the app.
- **Basic salary too low for the requested service.** Family visa for spouse + children requires a basic salary minimum (currently AED 4,000 with housing or AED 5,000 without). If the certificate shows less, you cannot sponsor regardless of total package.

## [H2] How the MOHRE certificate affects your gratuity

This is the under-appreciated point. Your gratuity (end-of-service benefit) under UAE Labour Law is calculated on your **basic salary as registered with MOHRE**, not on your total monthly package and not on what your offer letter says. The MOHRE salary certificate is the authoritative source of that number.

If you joined a company where the offer letter quoted AED 12,000 total but the MOHRE contract registered AED 5,000 basic + AED 5,000 housing + AED 2,000 transport, your gratuity will be calculated on AED 5,000 — not AED 12,000. For someone with 10 years of service, that is the difference between roughly AED 60,000 and AED 144,000 at the end.

This is why we recommend every UAE employee download their MOHRE salary certificate at least once a year and verify the basic salary line. Calculate your projected gratuity with the [Gratuity Calculator](/tools/gratuity-calculator/) using your actual basic salary, not your gross.

## [H2] Frequently Asked Questions

### [H3] Is the MOHRE salary certificate free?

Yes. Issuing the certificate through the MOHRE app or website is free. Typing centres may charge AED 20–30 for the service if you go through them.

### [H3] How long is a MOHRE salary certificate valid?

The certificate itself doesn't carry an expiry date, but most authorities require it to be issued within the last 30 days. Regenerate when needed.

### [H3] Can I get a MOHRE salary certificate in English only?

The standard certificate is bilingual Arabic + English. You usually cannot generate an English-only version, and you don't need to — UAE authorities accept the bilingual format.

### [H3] Does a free zone employee get a MOHRE salary certificate?

Not from MOHRE. Free zone employees are registered with their free zone authority (DIFC, DMCC, ADGM, JAFZA, etc.) and get the equivalent certificate from that authority instead. The MOHRE app will not generate one for free zone contracts.

### [H3] Can my employer block me from downloading my salary certificate?

No. The MOHRE app pulls directly from the live MOHRE contract record. As long as your work permit is active, you can generate the certificate without your employer's involvement.

### [H3] What if I have multiple jobs (limited part-time permits)?

If you have multiple active MOHRE permits — for example, a primary full-time job plus a part-time permit — the app will let you generate a certificate per employer. Pick the relevant one.
